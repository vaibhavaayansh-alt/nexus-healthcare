💉 Vaccination Guide

Understanding vaccines, their importance, and vaccination schedules for all ages.

Vaccine Schedule

Recommended Vaccination Schedule

General vaccination guidelines for different age groups. Always consult a healthcare provider for personalized advice.

Age Group Vaccines When to Get
Infants (0-12 months) BCG, Hepatitis B, DPT, Polio, Hib, Rotavirus, PCV Birth to 12 months
Children (1-5 years) MMR, Varicella, DPT Booster, Polio Booster, Hepatitis A 1 to 5 years
Children (6-12 years) HPV (Girls), Tdap Booster, Meningococcal 6 to 12 years
Adolescents (13-18 years) HPV, Meningococcal, Tdap, Flu Vaccine (Annual) 13 to 18 years
Adults (19-49 years) Flu (Annual), Tdap (Every 10 years), HPV (Up to 45), Hepatitis A/B 19 to 49 years
Seniors (50+ years) Flu (Annual), Pneumococcal, Shingles, Tdap (Every 10 years) 50+ years
Common Vaccines

Types of Vaccines

Learn about the most common vaccines and their importance.

💉 Preventive

BCG Vaccine

Protects against tuberculosis (TB), especially in children.

What it prevents: Tuberculosis (TB), a serious lung infection.

When to get: At birth or as early as possible in infancy.

Why it's important: TB is a major health concern in many parts of the world. BCG provides protection against severe forms of TB in children.

💉 Preventive

Hepatitis B Vaccine

Protects against hepatitis B virus, which affects the liver.

What it prevents: Hepatitis B, a viral infection that attacks the liver.

When to get: Birth, 1-2 months, and 6-18 months.

Why it's important: Hepatitis B can cause chronic liver disease and liver cancer. Vaccination provides long-term protection.

💉 Preventive

DPT Vaccine

Protects against diphtheria, pertussis (whooping cough), and tetanus.

What it prevents: Diphtheria, Pertussis (Whooping Cough), Tetanus.

When to get: 2, 4, 6, 15-18 months, and 4-6 years (booster).

Why it's important: These are serious bacterial infections that can be fatal. DPT vaccine provides comprehensive protection.

💉 Preventive

Polio Vaccine

Protects against poliovirus, which can cause paralysis.

What it prevents: Polio, a viral disease that can cause paralysis.

When to get: Birth, 6, 10, and 14 weeks; booster at 16-24 months.

Why it's important: Polio is a crippling disease. Vaccination has helped eliminate polio from most parts of the world.

💉 Preventive

MMR Vaccine

Protects against measles, mumps, and rubella.

What it prevents: Measles, Mumps, Rubella (German Measles).

When to get: 12-15 months and 4-6 years (booster).

Why it's important: These diseases can cause serious complications, especially in children. MMR provides safe and effective protection.

💉 Preventive

HPV Vaccine

Protects against human papillomavirus, which can cause cervical cancer.

What it prevents: HPV infection and related cancers (cervical, throat, etc.).

When to get: 9-14 years (2 doses); 15-26 years (3 doses).

Why it's important: HPV is a major cause of cervical cancer. Vaccination provides up to 90% protection against HPV-related cancers.

Why Vaccines

Why Vaccination Matters

Understanding the importance of vaccines for individual and community health.

🛡️

Protection

Vaccines train your immune system to fight diseases before they can harm you.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Community Immunity

When many people are vaccinated, it protects those who cannot be vaccinated.

💪

Disease Eradication

Vaccines have helped eradicate diseases like smallpox and reduce many others.

💰

Cost-Effective

Preventing diseases through vaccination is much cheaper than treating them.
