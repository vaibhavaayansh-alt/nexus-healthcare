Disease

Fever

Understanding fever, its causes, and when to seek medical attention.

What it is:

Fever is a temporary increase in body temperature, often due to an illness or infection. It's a natural defense mechanism.

Common signs:

Elevated body temperature (above 100.4°F / 38°C), chills, sweating, headache, muscle aches, weakness.

Prevention:

Practice good hygiene, stay hydrated, get adequate rest, and maintain a healthy immune system.

When to seek help:

If fever exceeds 103°F (39.4°C), lasts more than 3 days, or is accompanied by severe symptoms like difficulty breathing or confusion.

Disease

Common Cold

Learn about the common cold, its symptoms, and how to manage it.

What it is:

The common cold is a viral infection of the upper respiratory tract. It is one of the most frequent illnesses worldwide.

Common signs:

Runny or stuffy nose, sore throat, cough, sneezing, mild headache, and sometimes low-grade fever.

Prevention:

Wash hands frequently, avoid close contact with infected individuals, and maintain good overall health.

When to seek help:

If symptoms persist beyond 10 days, worsen, or are accompanied by high fever or difficulty breathing.

Disease

Diabetes Awareness

Understanding diabetes, its types, and management strategies.

What it is:

Diabetes is a chronic condition that affects how the body processes blood sugar (glucose). There are two main types: Type 1 and Type 2.

Common signs:

Increased thirst, frequent urination, extreme hunger, unexplained weight loss, fatigue, and blurred vision.

Prevention:

Maintain a healthy weight, eat a balanced diet, exercise regularly, and monitor blood sugar levels as advised.

When to seek help:

If you experience symptoms of diabetes or have risk factors, consult a healthcare professional for testing.

Disease

High Blood Pressure

Information about hypertension, its risks, and management.

What it is:

High blood pressure (hypertension) is a common condition where the force of blood against artery walls is consistently too high.

Common signs:

Often no symptoms. Severe cases may include headaches, shortness of breath, or nosebleeds.

Prevention:

Maintain a healthy weight, reduce salt intake, exercise regularly, limit alcohol, and manage stress.

When to seek help:

Regular checkups are essential. Seek immediate help if you experience chest pain or severe headache.

Disease

Asthma

Understanding asthma, triggers, and management strategies.

What it is:

Asthma is a chronic lung condition that inflames and narrows the airways, making breathing difficult.

Common signs:

Wheezing, shortness of breath, chest tightness, and coughing, especially at night or early morning.

Prevention:

Avoid triggers like allergens, smoke, and strong odors. Take prescribed medications as directed.

When to seek help:

Seek immediate help if symptoms worsen or if rescue medication doesn't provide relief.

Prevention

Heart Health

Tips for maintaining a healthy heart and preventing cardiovascular disease.

What it is:

Heart health refers to the overall condition of your heart and cardiovascular system. It's essential for overall wellbeing.

Common signs:

Regular checkups monitor blood pressure, cholesterol, and heart function. Chest pain or shortness of breath may indicate problems.

Prevention:

Eat a heart-healthy diet, exercise regularly, avoid smoking, manage stress, and maintain a healthy weight.

When to seek help:

Seek immediate medical attention for chest pain, shortness of breath, or sudden dizziness.

Nutrition

Nutrition

Understanding balanced nutrition and healthy eating habits for all ages.

What it is:

Nutrition is the process of consuming and utilizing food for growth, health, and energy. A balanced diet is essential.

Common signs:

Good nutrition leads to energy, healthy weight, strong immunity, and overall wellbeing. Poor nutrition may cause fatigue or illness.

Prevention:

Eat a variety of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins. Stay hydrated and limit processed foods.

When to seek help:

Consult a dietitian or healthcare provider for personalized nutrition advice.

First Aid

Dehydration

Recognizing and preventing dehydration for better health.

What it is:

Dehydration occurs when the body loses more fluids than it takes in, affecting normal body functions.

Common signs:

Thirst, dry mouth, dark urine, fatigue, dizziness, and confusion in severe cases.

Prevention:

Drink adequate water daily, increase intake during hot weather or physical activity, and eat water-rich foods.

When to seek help:

Seek immediate help if there is confusion, rapid heartbeat, or inability to keep fluids down.

Prevention

Hygiene

Personal and community hygiene practices to prevent infection.

What it is:

Hygiene refers to practices that maintain health and prevent the spread of disease through cleanliness.

Common signs:

Good hygiene practices include regular handwashing, oral care, bathing, and clean living spaces.

Prevention:

Wash hands frequently with soap, cover coughs, clean surfaces, and maintain personal cleanliness.

When to seek help:

If you develop persistent infections or notice signs of poor hygiene-related illness.

Prevention

Vaccination

Understanding vaccines, their importance, and vaccination schedules.

What it is:

Vaccination is a safe and effective way to protect against infectious diseases by building immunity.

Common signs:

Vaccines are given at specific ages. Mild side effects may include soreness or low-grade fever.

Prevention:

Follow recommended vaccination schedules for children and adults. Stay updated on boosters.

When to seek help:

Consult a healthcare provider about vaccination needs and any concerns.
