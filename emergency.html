🚑 Emergency & First Aid

General awareness, first aid guidance, and recognizing when to seek professional medical help.

🚨 In a Life-Threatening Emergency

Contact your local emergency services immediately or seek professional medical care. Do not wait. This website provides educational information only and is not a substitute for emergency medical services.

First Aid Awareness

Basic First Aid Guidance

Educational information for common first aid situations. Always seek professional medical help when needed.

🩹

Minor Cuts & Scrapes

Basic care for minor wounds to prevent infection.

  • Wash hands before touching the wound
  • Clean the wound with mild soap and water
  • Apply a clean bandage or dressing
  • Monitor for signs of infection
🔥

Minor Burns

First aid for minor burns and scalds.

  • Cool the burn with running cool water
  • Do not use ice or ice water
  • Cover with a sterile, non-stick dressing
  • Seek medical help for serious burns
😵

Fainting

What to do if someone faints or feels faint.

  • Lay the person flat on their back
  • Elevate feet slightly if possible
  • Loosen tight clothing
  • Seek medical help if they don't recover quickly
🦴

Sprains & Strains

Basic care for minor sprains and strains.

  • Rest the injured area
  • Apply ice (not directly on skin)
  • Compress with an elastic bandage
  • Elevate the injured area if possible
🩸

Nosebleeds

How to handle a nosebleed safely.

  • Sit upright and lean slightly forward
  • Pinch the nostrils together
  • Apply a cold compress to the nose bridge
  • Seek help if bleeding doesn't stop after 20 minutes
🌡️

High Fever

When to be concerned about fever.

  • Monitor temperature regularly
  • Stay hydrated and rest
  • Seek medical help if fever exceeds 103°F (39.4°C)
  • Seek immediate help for confusion or difficulty breathing
