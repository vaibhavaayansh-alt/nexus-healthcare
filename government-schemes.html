Public Healthcare

Government Healthcare Initiatives

Information about major Indian public healthcare schemes and programs.

⚠️ Important Note

This information is for educational purposes only. Eligibility criteria, benefits, and scheme rules may change. Always verify current information through official Government of India sources.

🏥 National Scheme

Ayushman Bharat

India's flagship public health insurance scheme providing coverage for eligible families.

Purpose: To provide health coverage for eligible families

Who it's for: Eligible families as per government criteria

General Benefit: Coverage for eligible hospitalization expenses

Learn More →
🛡️ National Scheme

PM-JAY

Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana - A component of Ayushman Bharat.

Purpose: To provide health coverage for eligible families

Who it's for: Eligible families as per government criteria

General Benefit: Coverage for eligible hospitalization expenses

Learn More →
🏥 Healthcare Infrastructure

Ayushman Arogya Mandirs

Healthcare centers providing comprehensive primary healthcare services.

Purpose: To provide accessible primary healthcare

Who it's for: All communities

General Benefit: Comprehensive primary healthcare services

🌍 National Mission

National Health Mission

A comprehensive healthcare mission improving health outcomes across India.

Purpose: To strengthen healthcare systems

Who it's for: All communities

General Benefit: Improved healthcare access and outcomes

👩 Maternal Health

Janani Suraksha Yojana

Maternal health program promoting institutional deliveries among pregnant women.

Purpose: To promote institutional deliveries

Who it's for: Pregnant women

General Benefit: Financial assistance for safe delivery

❤️ Regional Scheme

PMJAY SEHAT

Health coverage scheme for eligible families in specific regions.

Purpose: To provide health coverage

Who it's for: Eligible families in specific regions

General Benefit: Coverage for eligible treatments

✅ Always verify: Check official Government of India sources for current and accurate information about these schemes.
