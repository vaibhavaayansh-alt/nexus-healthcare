🇮🇳 India's Public Health Coverage Scheme

Ayushman Bharat — PM-JAY

"Understanding India's Public Health Coverage Scheme"

🏥 What is PM-JAY?

PM-JAY provides eligible families with health cover of up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for eligible secondary and tertiary hospitalization, subject to scheme rules.

⚠️ Important: This is health coverage, not cash. Eligible families can receive coverage for eligible hospitalization expenses at empanelled hospitals.

What is PM-JAY?

The Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) is a component of Ayushman Bharat, India's public health coverage scheme. It provides coverage for eligible families for secondary and tertiary hospitalization.

💰

What Does ₹5 Lakh Mean?

It means eligible families can receive health coverage up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for eligible hospitalization expenses at empanelled hospitals. It is not cash deposited into a bank account.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Who May Be Eligible?

Eligibility is determined by government-defined criteria. Families are identified based on specific parameters. Always check official sources for current eligibility information.

📍

Where Can People Get Help?

Care is available at empanelled hospitals, government healthcare facilities, and through official PM-JAY resources and authorized help channels.

⚠️ Important: Eligibility, benefits, hospital availability and scheme rules may change. Always verify current information through official government sources.
