Mental Health

Understanding Mental Health Conditions

Learn about common mental health conditions and how to recognize them.

😰 Anxiety

Anxiety Disorders

Understanding anxiety, its symptoms, and how to manage it.

What it is: Anxiety disorders involve excessive fear, worry, or nervousness that interferes with daily life.

Common signs: Restlessness, rapid heartbeat, difficulty concentrating, sleep problems, avoidance of certain situations.

Coping strategies: Deep breathing, meditation, talking to someone, physical exercise, professional help.

When to seek help: If anxiety interferes with daily activities or relationships.

😔 Depression

Depression

Understanding depression, its symptoms, and how to get support.

What it is: Depression is a mood disorder that causes persistent feelings of sadness, hopelessness, and loss of interest.

Common signs: Low mood, loss of interest, fatigue, changes in appetite, sleep issues, difficulty concentrating.

Coping strategies: Talk to someone, seek professional help, exercise, set small goals, practice self-care.

When to seek help: If feelings persist for more than two weeks or affect daily functioning.

😤 Stress

Stress Management

How to identify and manage stress effectively.

What it is: Stress is the body's response to pressure, demands, or challenging situations.

Common signs: Irritability, headaches, fatigue, difficulty sleeping, changes in appetite, tension.

Coping strategies: Deep breathing, mindfulness, physical activity, healthy eating, time management, talking to others.

When to seek help: When stress feels overwhelming or persistent.

🧘 Wellness

Emotional Wellness

Building emotional resilience and maintaining mental wellbeing.

What it is: Emotional wellness is the ability to understand, manage, and express your emotions effectively.

Benefits: Better relationships, improved mental health, greater life satisfaction, increased resilience.

Strategies: Practice self-compassion, build supportive relationships, set boundaries, seek joy, accept imperfection.

When to seek help: When you feel emotionally overwhelmed or struggling to cope.

Daily Tips

Mental Health Tips for Everyday Life

Simple daily practices to support your mental wellbeing.

🧘

Practice Mindfulness

Take 5-10 minutes daily to focus on your breath and the present moment.

🏃

Stay Active

Even 15 minutes of physical activity can boost your mood and reduce stress.

😴

Get Enough Sleep

Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep each night for better mental health.

🗣️

Talk to Someone

Don't keep feelings bottled up — talk to trusted friends, family, or a professional.

📵

Take Breaks

Step away from screens and take regular breaks throughout the day.

🙏

Practice Gratitude

Write down three things you're grateful for each day to shift your perspective.
