👶 Child Health

Comprehensive health information for children from infancy to adolescence.

Child Health

Understanding Child Health Issues

Learn about common childhood health concerns, nutrition, and developmental milestones.

🍼 Infant Health

Infant Care Basics

Essential care tips for newborns and infants.

What it is: Infant care involves feeding, sleeping, hygiene, and developmental support for babies from birth to 12 months.

Key aspects: Breastfeeding or formula feeding, regular checkups, vaccinations, safe sleep practices, and tummy time.

Nutrition tips: Exclusive breastfeeding for first 6 months, introducing solid foods after 6 months, balanced diet for growth.

When to seek help: If the baby shows signs of illness, feeding difficulties, or developmental delays.

🧒 Child Development

Growth Milestones

Understanding developmental milestones for children from 1 to 12 years.

What it is: Developmental milestones are skills or behaviors that most children achieve by a certain age in areas like motor skills, language, and social development.

Key milestones: Walking, talking, social play, school readiness, reading, writing, and problem-solving skills.

Tips: Encourage play, read together, provide a supportive environment, and track growth.

When to seek help: If the child shows delays in reaching milestones or has developmental concerns.

🥗 Nutrition

Nutrition for Children

Essential nutrients and healthy eating tips for growing children.

What it is: Children need a balanced diet rich in proteins, carbohydrates, healthy fats, vitamins, and minerals for proper growth and development.

Essential nutrients: Protein (for growth), calcium (for bones), iron (for blood), vitamin D (for immunity), and fiber (for digestion).

Tips: Include fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins, and dairy. Limit sugary drinks and processed foods.

When to seek help: If the child shows poor appetite, weight loss, or nutritional deficiencies.

🤒 Childhood Illnesses

Common Childhood Illnesses

Understanding common illnesses in children and how to manage them.

What it is: Children commonly experience illnesses like cold, cough, fever, ear infections, and stomach infections due to developing immunity.

Common signs: Fever, cough, runny nose, diarrhea, vomiting, irritability, loss of appetite, or fatigue.

Tips: Ensure proper hygiene, keep the child hydrated, provide rest, and consult a doctor when needed.

When to seek help: If the child has high fever, difficulty breathing, or persistent symptoms.

Milestones

Child Development Milestones

Key developmental milestones to watch for as your child grows.

👶

Birth - 6 Months

Smiling, cooing, lifting head, rolling over, tracking objects with eyes.

🧸

6 - 12 Months

Sitting up, crawling, babbling, responding to name, playing peek-a-boo.

🚶

12 - 24 Months

Walking, saying first words, pointing to objects, following simple directions.

👧

2 - 4 Years

Running, jumping, speaking in sentences, playing with others, toilet training.

📚

4 - 6 Years

Reading readiness, counting, writing letters, making friends, following rules.

🧑

6 - 12 Years

Reading, writing, math skills, sports, independence, building friendships.
