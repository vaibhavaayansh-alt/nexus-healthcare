Youngest Healthcare Platform Founder

At just 13 years old, I built Nexus Healthcare — a complete web-based digital health platform to spread healthcare awareness.

My Journey
I am a student developer from Muzaffarpur, Bihar, currently studying in Class 8 at Shemford Futuristic School. My passion for technology and coding started at a young age, and I believe that innovation can solve real-world problems.

Why Nexus Healthcare?
I created Nexus Healthcare to connect people with better healthcare information and resources. This project is my way of contributing to society and making healthcare awareness accessible to everyone — especially in rural and underserved communities. From designing the website to writing every line of code, I have built this platform from scratch.

My Vision
I want to use technology to make a positive impact on people's lives. Whether it's coding a website, learning new technologies, or working on meaningful projects, I am always eager to grow, learn, and build something that truly matters.
