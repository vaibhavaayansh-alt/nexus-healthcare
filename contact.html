Find answers to common questions about Nexus Healthcare.

What is Nexus Healthcare? ▼

Nexus Healthcare is an educational healthcare-awareness platform created as a technology project to help people understand and access better healthcare information. We provide resources on health topics, government schemes, and community healthcare.

Is the health information provided reliable? ▼

We provide educational and awareness information. However, Nexus Healthcare is an educational project and does not replace professional medical advice. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical concerns.

Can I book a real appointment through this website? ▼

No. The appointment feature on our Doctors page is a demonstration for educational purposes only. No actual appointments are being booked. Please contact real healthcare providers for medical services.
