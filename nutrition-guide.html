Nutrition

Understanding Essential Nutrients

Learn about the essential nutrients your body needs for optimal health.

💪 Macronutrients

Proteins

Understanding the importance of protein for growth and repair.

What it is: Protein is a macronutrient essential for building and repairing tissues, making enzymes, and supporting immune function.

Sources: Meat, fish, eggs, dairy, legumes, nuts, seeds, and tofu.

Daily needs: Approximately 0.8g per kg of body weight for adults; more for growing children, athletes, and seniors.

Tips: Include protein in every meal to maintain muscle mass and overall health.

⚡ Macronutrients

Carbohydrates

Understanding the role of carbohydrates as the body's primary energy source.

What it is: Carbohydrates are the body's main source of energy. They are broken down into glucose, which fuels your cells.

Sources: Whole grains, fruits, vegetables, legumes, and dairy products.

Daily needs: Carbohydrates should make up about 45-65% of your daily calorie intake.

Tips: Choose complex carbohydrates (whole grains, vegetables) over simple sugars.

🧈 Macronutrients

Healthy Fats

Understanding the importance of healthy fats for brain and heart health.

What it is: Fats are essential for brain function, hormone production, and absorbing fat-soluble vitamins.

Sources: Avocado, nuts, seeds, olive oil, fatty fish, and coconut oil.

Daily needs: Fats should make up about 20-35% of your daily calorie intake.

Tips: Choose unsaturated fats over saturated and trans fats for better heart health.

🥬 Micronutrients

Vitamins

Understanding the role of vitamins in maintaining overall health and immunity.

What it is: Vitamins are organic compounds essential for various bodily functions, including immunity, vision, and bone health.

Key vitamins: Vitamin A (vision), Vitamin C (immunity), Vitamin D (bone health), Vitamin E (antioxidant), Vitamin K (blood clotting).

Sources: Fruits, vegetables, dairy, meat, and sunlight (for Vitamin D).

Tips: Eat a colorful variety of fruits and vegetables to get a wide range of vitamins.

🦴 Micronutrients

Minerals

Understanding the importance of minerals for bones, muscles, and overall health.

What it is: Minerals are inorganic elements essential for bone health, muscle function, and various bodily processes.

Key minerals: Calcium (bones), Iron (blood), Magnesium (muscles), Potassium (heart), Zinc (immunity).

Sources: Dairy, leafy greens, nuts, seeds, meat, and legumes.

Tips: Include a variety of mineral-rich foods in your diet to meet daily needs.

💧 Hydration

Water & Hydration

Understanding the importance of staying hydrated for overall health.

What it is: Water is essential for regulating body temperature, transporting nutrients, and removing waste.

Daily needs: Aim for about 8 glasses (2 liters) of water per day, more if active or in hot weather.

Signs of dehydration: Thirst, dark urine, fatigue, dizziness, and dry skin.

Tips: Carry a water bottle, eat water-rich foods, and drink water throughout the day.
